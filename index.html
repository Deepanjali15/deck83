About

Deck 83 is a music player styled like an 80s cassette deck, with spinning reels and a purple spectrum meter.

It comes with three built-in chiptune tracks. Add your own audio files and they appear in your playlist, ready to play, skip or delete.

How it works

Create a free account with your email, then log in from any device. Your songs are stored on the device where you add them, so they never get uploaded.

FAQ

Do I need an account to listen?
Yes. Creating one takes a minute and only needs an email and a password.
Will my songs show up on my other devices?
No. Your account works everywhere, but songs stay on the device you added them on.
Can I play Spotify songs?
No. Deck 83 plays the built-in tracks and audio files from your own device.
Which file types work?
Any audio your browser can play, such as MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG.
